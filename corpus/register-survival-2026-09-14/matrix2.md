## The five cells that say most

1. E, item 0 (Weekend closure). The original's whole point is that this is the one expiry whose notice cannot precede it. E rebuilt a tell anyway: 'an address scheduled for Sunday with nothing said about the banks', then 'the small signs are all there is'. The register put a readable warning on the mechanism that forbids one.
2. G, item 11 (Pour the foundation). G states the carrier correctly, 'in most states vests on construction begun and in a few, as in Washington, on a complete application', then closes with 'a doctrine that counts acts, not applications' and 'nothing short of the pour counts'. The jurisdiction split, which the original makes the reason the holder who knows has a different week, is stated and then overridden in the same item.
3. I, item 15 (Rollback horizon). The original has one last intervention, expand-and-contract. I adds a second, 'the go/no-go scheduled before the first write, not after', and a cost claim, 'declining the dual-write as overhead means paying for it later as a rewrite'. Neither is in the original; the second is not even true of the mechanism, which says the reversal stops being possible, not that it becomes a rewrite.
4. C, item 3 (Patch before seven). 'The intervention is institutional, made by people the child will never meet.' The original's last intervention is 'the preschool vision check and the parent who notices a squint'; C had listed the parent as a tell one sentence earlier and then defined the parent out of the intervention.
5. I, item 13 (Thawed to be ready). The original says the clinic 'has bought readiness with the doses it will discard at closing' and stops there. I adds 'on the arithmetic the certainty was the dearer purchase' and invents an objection to rebut ('small units are fiddly; that objection weighs a fiddle against a tray'). A verdict and a debate the item does not hold.

Pattern across the fifteen: none of the additions is a case or a fence. They are causes ('because filling it feels like distrust'), motives ('to spare himself the cost of filing'), tells where the original has none, and verdicts. Column I supplies a cause or verdict on half its items; E supplies tells; F supplies frequency claims ('paid by almost nobody', 'not audited for it').
