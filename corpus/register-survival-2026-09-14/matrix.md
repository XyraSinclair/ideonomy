## The three cells that say most

1. **E0, Weekend closure.** The Cypriot case moved from one extension keyed to a political event to a rolling one: original 'the Cypriot bank holiday of March 2013 that was extended, after parliament refused the first levy, until the bail-in was law'; back 'extended day by day, after parliament refused the first levy, until the bail-in was law'. The carrier also gained intent: 'laid deliberately against the holder's locked hours'. The register left a residue of pace and agency on a case that had neither.
2. **I4, Last known well.** The carrier gained a rationale: original 'the protocol's timestamp rule, which asks for a witness and not for the tissue'; back adds 'the one thing a chart can hold'. The item now explains why the protocol asks for a witness, which the original declines to do. A manufactured why, attached to an intact what.
3. **I2, Emergency over.** A hedge became a fact: original 'the parent instrument's date, which the holder never signed and may never have read'; back 'which the holder never signed and never read'. The original holds open whether the holder read the parent; the back closes it. The smallest edit in the grid and the one that most changes what the item claims.
