Pointers to Internet Resources about Ideonomy

Not much is available in the internet about Ideonomy or Patrick Gunkel. These are some of the links that you might want to follow to get a feel for what is available out there. - 10/25/97
 
  • http://www.parc.xerox.com/spl/members/saund/interests.html
      • Eric Saund works at Xerox Palo Alto Research Center. At the very bottom of his interests page, there is a line that he has reserved a space 'for Pat Gunkel, founder of the science of Ideonomy'
       
  • http://www.iath.virginia.edu/lists_archive/Humanist/v05/0074.html
