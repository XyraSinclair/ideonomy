It is easy to imagine a system wherein tissues succeeded one another as flat
Plates or beads on a string in alternating series of bisignal effects upon one
another or at least where nonalternating succession occurred, having their value
because local complex interactions would be enabled within each stage giving an
important independence to the stages and enabling a serial hierarchy,

It has been proposed that central corollary discharge is the basis of
So-called voluntary movement for the reason that anticipatory images are shunted
to sensory and motor stages prior to or along with enaction, so that prior,
simltaneous, directly following, long following, or just ‘pictorial’ information
of this motion is available for such purposes as mirroring processes, telling
consequences well, refining actions for consequences, refining perceptions for
actions or consequences, or virtually converting perceptions into models of
Consequences or perceptions whose lesser concern is sensory. It may as well be
Suggested that the superposition of extra and successive frontal cortices
anteriorly has served a simple process of storage of motor and sensorimotor
images or a picture of action, which then evolve into elegant schema’ or at least
or first into crude system whose possibilities are capable of shortecircuiting
and otherwise modifying the more posterior motor processes; the addition of
basal ganglionic inhibition as a loop from, say, the more anterior to the more
posterior motor cortices could afford these evolving complex, systematic, and
elegant action patterns an opportunity of managing in time and space the
previously and subsequently evolved more posterior motor patterns (there is no
particular reason, perhaps, for dichotomizing the more anterior from the more
posterior motor cortices in terms of “voluntariness", for the more associated
patterns of the former lack only the specificity of the latter isolated in terms
of individual mscles; the best argument for the “voluntariness" of any centers
concerned with action is no doubt the complexity of structure and function
characterizing cortic macroneuroanatomy and microanatomy in contrast to subcortic
centers).

It's conceivable that the function of agranular cortices is stereotyped
behavior (resonance) whereas granular cortices would involve habituative and

cortices could be put in control of the former via the basal ganglia &). It

It should not. be surprising that motor or more anterior cortices (or analogous
subcortic centers) should be concerned with serial things for in these tissues
processes of selection and combination of the static and fixed (passive) memories
more preoccupied with sensation and perception of the impersonally active or
externally independent would be dominant. Nor should such serial concern be
surprising in the neighborhood of the hippocampus for there motor information
converges, transistentialiam might operate, and regulations of drive would
involve distinct choices of percepts and even acts and their systematic arrangement
in time (in parallel, in succession, in alternation, in temporal modulation, or

temporal patterns of conflict and succession in sten equilibria or other arrange~
ments. It is conceivable that the return of information from the frontal cortex
and convergence from the cortex generally to the temporal lobe, perhaps in
independent interaction with the drive mechanisms (highly spread in time and
thus causing diffuse Signifigance or irrejective organization of presential
