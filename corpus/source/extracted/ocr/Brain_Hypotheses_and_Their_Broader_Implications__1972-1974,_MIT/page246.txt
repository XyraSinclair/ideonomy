Corticle, Wendell Krieg's term for a cell column in the cortex. I suggested
"precorticle" might refer to a column or nidulus in one area contributing to
a column or nidulus in another area, a "postcorticle”, terms which might ease
some discussions.

Ideomorphosis, At an early stage, recognizing the tendency to think of ideas
as distinct from sensa or mere engrams, I suggested this term for the process
or processes of or for the development of ideas, since it also suggests their
formality.

Decussation. I used this word in a broad and umsual way to refer to the vergence
of connexions between nervous regions, especially cortic areas.

Cortices. The plural of cortex, I and others loosely refer to defined or vague
areas Within a cortex seperated horizontally, vertically, or functionally as
"cortices"; the term also refers to different cortices such. neocortex, paleccortex,
cerebellocortez, corticoid nuclei, &.
