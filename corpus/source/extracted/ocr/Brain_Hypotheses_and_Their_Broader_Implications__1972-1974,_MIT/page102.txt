THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN CHIMP VS HUMAN BABIES
Pat Gunkel

The obvious difference that a human baby might have to a chimp baby might be,
to guess without knowing, in the quality of the human's control over its
attentional processes, say apparent as a stronger, more persistent, more
intelligent gaze and a correlative suppression over otherwise interferent
systems such as motor, emotional, or other sensory processes. Then from whence
consciousness and that which we see as attention? Fran development from simple
reactions to stimuli, orienting acts, and stabilities. It may be supposed that
sensory cortices are strengthened in their control over themselves or of lower
reticular, behavioral, or motor processes, perhaps particularly the highest
sensory cortices, or that reticular arousal of these cortices (however this
classical notion is done’) is increased or better located in these cortices
(as to layer, cell, synapse). Ideas of this kind might be served in various
wayse One would be for motor control by these cortices to increase, eg
preempting lower distractions or competitions, making action simpler, more
exact, more powerful, faster, potentially more complex, more reversible, more
correlated with sensory processes, more integrated in all behavior, more
experimental (action as perception), more comprehensible, more voluntary,
more progressive, more elegant (anticipatory and generalizing), &; this is
