Allocation. The distribution of informational functions over different neurons.

Cell fidelities. At least until such time as the "receptive fields" and
“receptive field properties" of different celis and classes of cells, their
degree of permanence » their mode of origin and basis, their inventory and
relations, their determinism and universality, their quantitative characteristics,
their populations, and their functional circumstances have been ascertained,

I advise using the term "cell fidelities" in place of more absolute concepts

and terms. Thus the term is noncomittal,.

Wiring. Given our sheer ignorance about specific » general, types of, and
integral functional or anatomic connexions in the brain, and the fact that
apparent patterns may actually involve purely functional organization of ideas
&¢, but also given our need for referring to some pattern of "connexion", I
propose we use in this qualified sense the term “wiring". Ie the term should
stand for whatever organization in the tissue lends specificity to mental
function. Very elementary or very diffuse organization may confer a surprising
amount of specificity, determinism, and order upon such function, and it is
not absolutely necessary to conjecture greater "wiring", whose complexity may
even be detrimental to analysis, However, it may be that types of mind and
intellect could or do only arise by virtue of an intrinsically ingenious
circuitry whose unravelment may have to precede explanation of the brain or
any brain.

Noogeny. The hypothetical or real origin and development of any mind, in whole
or part (eg of a mental function abstracted from its setting). There is a very
conspicuous need for this term,

Analogic. Initially I used this term, as many do, as a happier substitute for
“analogue*. But I am inclined to think it is too readily confused with mere
analogy to be workable and have had recourse to the original term.

Resonance. Sans sharp boundary in actual fact, nervous activity that is in some
basie sense static in space. Thus the alteration of the state of a neuron
could lead to persistent characteristic activity. This might itself ba the or
a type of memory, or the emissions, modulations, and interactions might serve
to condition the tissue to remember the involved trace. Sometimes the term is
used to refer to a type of cireulation where extremely local flows are extremely
or basically regular, or where activity is confined within the borders of some
tissue, where a prolongation of activity may in this way lead to longterm
memorization through the intervention of basic metabolie or proteosynthetic
processes$ this might be called "reverberation",

Circulation. Opposed to resonance, in a true sense, is the actual spatial
movement (translation, rotation, and displacement) of engrams over tissue. ‘The
information may or may not, initially or finally, be distributed.

Transinformation. This is a very general term, used by others, referring to
exchange and interchange of information over other compartments of the brain,
as intereellularly or intercolumnarly, and connoting its mere propagation or
universalization, and the fact of the survival of information after many
transformations and translocations.

Bidomtation. Going beyond small particles of information, there may occur and
there may be special value in the preservation of the complete pattern of
information, but this complete pattern or “image” may be indifferent to major
transformations in space, time, function, and structure,
