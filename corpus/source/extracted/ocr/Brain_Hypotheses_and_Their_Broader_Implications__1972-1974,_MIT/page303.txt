Some questions are about the structure of the input to a cell, the cause
and nature of the complexity of output histograms, and the extent to which
the complex inputs, outputs, and-aileslanses of a cell are functions of
extracellular circuitry and the nature of this control. The stability of
responses may be quantitated in various ways and considered as to the above.
It seems rather clear that the diameter and other measurements of axons will
govern the not only pulse velocity but also the efficacy and specific effect
of the terminal pulse, and all these things need to be systematically
determined. The interspike intervals of histograms need measurement and
then expression in terms of the number, distribution, and efficacy of the
total pulses received by cells per burst-to-burst and intra-burst epoch; eg
the frequency-density may be such that cells respond as they do to large
and interesting spatiotemporal clusters, or there may be phenomena of
"wasted queues” where only a few pulses of hundreds may fire and take the
cell, “leaders” that may regularly take the cell by some economy or ideal,
"successive leaders” with regular drifts, "mixers" freer of order, &. One
can imagine isoporie and anisoporic "coders" and "hoops" that could be
"troglodytic, vagabondish, or escapistic’. Another question pertains to
the spatiotemporal combinations that could fire a cell, variously, and their
souree of regularity; eg possibly sets of same-twig, interbranchlet,
interbranch, & EPSPs (even with ISPs) will alone or specifically in
combination fire the cell, and equilibrial functions and structures will
be set up or above between cells (see my list of equilibria under #67 in
“order in Experience”), There may be a possibility and a dominance of
a eertain quota, distribution, and timing of pulses throughout or over an
arbor leading to peculiar summative firing or potentiation. Furthermore,
cells in tissue may have worked out extreme efficiencies for achieving
various effects. The raison d'etre for the quantitative and qualitative
organization of spikes and bursts may be practical cell firing and,
paradoxically, pewrougrene organization. The specific patterns of an
individual cell's firings may trigger, control, or contribute to 10,000
cells in the distance. What determines latency, intensity, frequency,
sensitivity, reliability, variability, regularity, interval, complexity,
specific organization, trend, probability, & of firing-—-mainly, miitiply,
totally, little, not at all, cooperatively, and just how?
