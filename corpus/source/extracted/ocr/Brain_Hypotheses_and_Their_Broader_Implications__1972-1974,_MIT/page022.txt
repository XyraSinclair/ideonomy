The poet in history was often an adviser to kings, and to whom does one
go to understand the meaning of life but the poet? The poet is concerned
with the flight of things and the image of the world is alate. It is the
expanse, complexity, and specificity of the world that is the source, the
reference, and the terminus of ideas or the real form of things for things

yet experience is ever incomplete and the things that fall within this
truncated experience are arrows that point to horizons infinitely distant
that cannot be brought within the present as parts thereof, so that the poet
is merely concerned with the vastest intercomparison of these arrows and
what he utters thereupon cannot be rewritten else it will point elsewhere,
