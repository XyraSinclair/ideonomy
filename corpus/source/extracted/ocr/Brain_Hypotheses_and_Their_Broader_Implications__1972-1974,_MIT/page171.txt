IS MAN THE PRODUCT OF ACCELERATED OR SPECIALIZED EVOLUTION?
Pat Gunkel

Phere are good reasons for thinking that man is the product of evolut
acceleration and/or specialization. There are quite a number of ways in which
this might be so. Royal polygyny or promiscuity in which he at the top of the
pecking order, there by virtue of his superior prowess, propagates his kind
with particularly heavy stamp. Intellectual survival and competition, by which
is meant an enhanced dependency on one's brains, conceivably further enhanced
by specific deficit in other physical or mental ability. Progressive regression
of inherited behavior forcing versatility and innovation and possibly some sort
of cultural continuity. Purposeful intraspecific interindividual aggression
selectively multiplying ingenuity. The emergence of personality and sufficient
intelligence that mating expresses judgment and intellectual affinity. Possibly
such organization of the brain that pleasure arises in and depends on mental
fulfilment and cortic solution of problems besetting the subcortex or the cortex.
Early language and tool using specializing the nervous system for interpersonal
communication, and perhaps therewith selection through expansion of particular
populations, through wars, and through what might be called intrigue or a differential
survival of the wittiest conversants and the most abstract insights. Intense
specialization by virtue of the decisive importance of some state, such as
speech, suddenly polarizing other considerations for organization of the nervous
system about itself. Social attitude could preferentially bachelorize the less
intelligent and intellectually (eg conversationally) entertaining and, this process
repeated for millenja across vast population, a slow continuous average of better
men from better animals might emerge. In terms of reason, creativity, and speech,
it is conceivable that never before had evolution a nervous specialization with
such general survival significance so that the entirety of the nervous system and
even the body could be organized incrementally and revolutionarily in pure service
to its cause; this might mean that the measure of distance in which man stands to
his compresent animal relations or his original ancestors is uniquely hidden in a
conflux of minute change or in an indistinguishable specialization, or that certain
kinds of change, hitherto unimportant, were for once engaged.

This is said by way of apology for one has the impression that our humanity may
represent an integral change of the image of our brain recalcitrant to both scientific
report and understanding: those changes which we document, and which even seem to
Support theories, may only distract us from the reintegration of the whole in the
step to man or in the long promenade through the streets of animal evolution, and
may moreover be but undirected or irrelevant concomitants of decisive change
elsewhere, eg embodied in the reformation of social life.

Another process which is of some interest is the possible role of unconscious
mental guidance in the answering of dysgenic tendencies so that through centuries
the racial stock has not declined even in its parts so much as one would think likely.
