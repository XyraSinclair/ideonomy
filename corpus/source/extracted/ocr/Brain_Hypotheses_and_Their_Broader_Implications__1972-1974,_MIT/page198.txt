Connect a given neuron (tape) with many others (10-1047) (locally, distantly,
or hierarchically) (the connections might be made so as to vary ephemeral ly
or semipermanently with use, eg facilitatively, disfacilitatively, or cum

(ie circular tapes) play into each other all at once so as to all record each
other's current and cumulative "song''. Perhaps the recordings should be
fluvial so that salient chronological patterns (''structural melodies'') will,
in effect, be strongest-deposited and "matric'' for less common patterns in
sculpturing the neuronal songs. (But note the interneuronal rates may be

interneuronal connections are not intended as simple amplifiers; the flux

held down to an unknown single and sum ratio to the neuron's own song.
(Inhibitory compresent neurons may prove desirable. )

Series of neurons (like intraareal and interareal series in brains) may
effectively down-cascade vivid experience into vivid memory ina hierarchy
of memory tapes.

Any number of sensory, motor, cognitive, emotive, &c areas are possible.

The "motor area" should combine "homeomorphic'' mappings of an effector's
anatomic" and "physiologic ranges (this kinesthesia and touch should be
well-represented, eg by different identifying timbres). Note that the variations
in coherence and incoherence of the neuronal tapes vis-a-vis each other
could be a means of exploring the infinity-dimensional world. Motor efferents
would run from these neurons' tapes as above.

The tissue could be trained to emit standard patterns, to intertransform
and to generalize same, and in this way to integratively and experimentally
Spontaneously evolve holistic behavioral melodies and, eventually,
conceptions relating thereto. A "hippocampal" area might be set up to
enable oneiric internal evolution of higher insights from organized memories.
