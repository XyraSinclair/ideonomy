If it is both feasible and justified--and it is unlikely to be either--the
Van Allen Radiation Belts of the Earth may eventually, as has been proposed,
be ''swept up'', ie removed, perhaps by 2005 or 2040. These belts consist of
protons, electrons, deuterons, and tritons of diverse energy, and are said
to have the form of a doughnut, exist around the equator, and extend for about
1.1 to 9 Terran radii. Since the proposal was made many years ago one assumes
it might have reflected a naive fear that these belts would have characteristics
incompatible with, or dangerous to, human beings passing through them or orbiting
the Earth. It is possible that exploding nuclear bombs, inserting matter,
altering the exosphere, creating titanic artificial electrical ér magnetic fields,
using catalyses strategically on the basis of a great deal of knowledge, Erc
might eventually enable the Belts to be temporarily, periodically, or permanently
destroyed, reduced, repositioned, restructured, altered spectrally, overloaded
or rarefied, reconstituted, modulated, &r the like. The cost, means, characteristics,
procedure, &¢ are unpredictable. Important questions must be answered first
such as the rate of restoration, the structure and dynamics of the Belts, the
lethal fluctuations (for example during Solar storms), the value of the Belts
and their states for climate and life on Earth (the function of the Belts),
the irreversible consequences or side effects of tampering, the negotiability or
circumnavigability of the Belts, the relocatability of future geosatellites,
the insulability of satellites and ships, &c. The real justification for removing
or altering the Belts may only appear as a massive, extensive, and permanent
Geosatellosphere arises during the first half of the 2]st century, or beyond.
Yet the Geosatellosphere might be inessential, or might learn how to protect itself
by creating intense local magnetic &r electrical fields by way of insulation, or
by using the surface-volume law to reduce the relative cost of sufficient surficial
insulation for structures of sufficient magnitude, a la Gerard O'Neil]. If it
is discovered that the Belts catalyze or otherwise control global climate, their
intensification, reduction, or other modification might be motivated by climatic
engineering needs on Earth. There may also be value in altering the Belts for
purely experimental reasons, at some future time.
