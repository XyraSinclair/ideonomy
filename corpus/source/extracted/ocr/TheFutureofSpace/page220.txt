SOLIAN. Of or relating to the (our) Solar System and that contained by the
heliopause or bounded by the systems of neighboring stars; of or relating
to Solian civilization, that culture which exists and will grow within the
Solar System, its various planetaries, vacua, and star. This term is proposed
because of the double meaning of "'solar'', which refers also to the Sun as
distinct from the bodies and vacua which surround it.

SOLAR. Of or relating to the Sun, our star.

EXTRASOLIAN. Of or relating to that beyond our Solar System, either as limited
by the heliopause or by the orbit of Pluto; of or relating to cultures, beings,
civilizations, messages, transportations, &rc beyond present or future Solian
Civilization.

SOLITERRAN = HELIOTERRAN. Of or relating to the Sun and Earth together. This
might be used to refer to the Lagrangian points between these 2 bodies, to

region limited externally by the orbit of Earth, to power transmission from

interspace plus the nearsides of the two planetaries, or to just the

(cislunar Space).

INTERTERRAN. Of, relating to, or involving 2 or more points on the Earth, eg the

COSMOPLASTIC. Of or involving that able to shape, or in fact shaping, a world or
the universe. This refers, in particular, to planeturgy and also to so-called
infinite cellular automata possessing the ability to engineer or reform the whole
universe. The main idea is in terms of shaping what is present in some form already.

COSMOPOIETIC. Of or involving that able to create, or in fact creating, a world
or the universe. Again this has particular reference to both planeturgy and
infinite cellular automata. In contradistinction to "cosmoplastic''’, the main
idea here is in terms of shaping or creating things ab initio or ex nihilo.
