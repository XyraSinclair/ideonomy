REASONS FOR SPACE

Maintains an important frontier: geographic, vectorial, scientific, technical,
economic, intellectual, spiritual, emotional, characteral, vis-a-vis rest
of the world, demographic, plutographic (ie in terms of natural resources),
and in terms of the human adventure generally.

Stimulates the economy: industrial employment, forces innovation, increases
excellence of workforce, triggers progressive policies, expands opportunity,
lubricates whole economy, benefits peripheral and ulterior firms,
advances general economic practises and attitudes (ie evolves the whole
economy), spins off things (abstract knowledge, processes, techniques,
products, materials, equipment, projects, programs, methods, &c), favors
the US over the world, benefits the whole world, &c. Accelerates change,
retires the obsolete and irrelevant, variegates and evolves industries
and businesses, increases overall economic organization, replaces military
waste, beneficially follows wars, underlines the future importance of
applied and pure science, de-vulgarizes wealth, encourages long-term and
non-zero sum investment, &c.

Revives, perpetuates, strengthens, enlarges, generalizes, differentiates,
vindicates, redirects, exemplifies, and improves the factual or fictitious
"American spirit'': enterprise, courage, determination, innovation,
growth, progress, perfection, technique, ambition, the romance of being
challenged, activity, leadership, idealism, self-sufficiency, cooperation,
industry, discovery, profit (economic gain, pursuit of a higher standard
of living), transcendence of obstacles, mastery over nature, mystical
futurism, savings and investment, development of resources, technical and
scientific superbity, curiosity, love of freedom, achievement-oriented
values, self-transcendence, self-dissatisfaction, ''maximalism'', poetry, &c.

Safeguards the United States, democracy, the human race, all posterity,
civilization, all of life, &c: by creating ''redundant earths'', diversified
being, greater civilization, the future in the present (ie compressing
future-historical development), less militarily vulnerable or interesting
civilization, a panhuman theme, panhuman and novel institutions (economy,
government, culture, science, overlapping ownership, synergistic patterns,
higher orientations, &c), an outer-directed psychology, &c; the United
States can surpass or equal her adversaries in Space, capitalism and
democracy can be fostered in Space, &c.

Enables an intrinsically valuable, non zero sum nonterran civilization to
start and evolve itself independently, yet a civilization that will
ultimately benefit Earth tremendously (economically, culturally, technically,
and even politically): people can exist which do not now exist, the
civilization in Space will ultimately become greater (eventually infinitely
greater) than Earth in economic, demographic, and cultural (or all other)
terms.

Inspires, entertains, elevates, preoccupies, reassures, &¢ mankind and its
divisions on and off Earth.

Expands, or will expand, human resources: minerals, energy, land, space, means,
knowledge, environments, opportunities, men, &c.

Has religious, aesthetic, educational, legal, &c¢ significance.

That achieved in Space so far encourages a greater investment of human resources
in Space.

Military needs and opportunities.

Astronomic benefits.

Destiny or ultimate things: the beckoning universe.

Peace: Space defeats or discourages war, unifies or elevates and redirects the
race, &c.
