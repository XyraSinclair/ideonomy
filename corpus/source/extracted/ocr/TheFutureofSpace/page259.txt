planetaries apt, probably or possibly, to differ from the mining situation
on Earth, now and in the future (10, 25, 50, 75, 100, 150... years hence)?

Relative to Earth, how accessible are minerals apt to be on these bodies?

What massive deposits are we apt to find on these planetaries?

What small but precious deposits, equally competitive?

To what extent are there alleged, possible, or probable present or future
resources shortages, or noncompetitive situations, on Earth?

To what extent is Terrestrial] wealth competitively enlargeable by further
Survey using old/future methods in un/usual areas, greater energy,
old/future methods of extraction/synthesis/use, resource substitution or
omission, &c?

perfectly efficient?

or future (reflecting economies of scale) costs of transport and all other
operations?

long-term development of Space?

ls the solar system apt to be Fractionated in any ideal ways?
