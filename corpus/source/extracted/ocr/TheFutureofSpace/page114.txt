The planet Mercury will be mined on some more than exploratory scale by
2010. Its proximity to the Sun, 36,000,000 miles with an eccentricity of 0.2,
and its lack of an atmospheric cover, which exposes the surface to the full
corpuscular bombardment of the Sun, might seem to render it of little interest
to mining and too inhospitable to exploration and development in any case.
