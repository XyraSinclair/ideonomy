as challenging and intrinsically fascinating places, however exotic and rigorous.
Outer space is especially challenging and attractive because it demands totally

new thinking, personal abandon, the synthesis of all essentials and luxuries,

and it promises the ultimate and almost ecstatic pleasure of having uniquely
achieved such things or of playing a role in their attainment by allying oneself
with transcendent destiny. The dwellings one should properly visualize as

arising in Space, say geosatellitically or at Gerard K. O'Neill's L-5 colonies,

are ultramodern and beautiful ones along the lines of the visionary cities of

the architect Paoli Soleri; much of their beauty will arise from sheer awareness

of nature's miraculous order and provision even in terms of such spiritual

entities as Spacial cities, and from pride and wonder over transcendent human
accomplishment. One's own creation is always cause for wonder and satisfaction.

The current state of science and technology will probably make inevitable a

high degree of comfort, room, efficiency, reliability, safety, environmental
interest, beauty, work and play, &c in such uranian habitats. Life in them

will have its sublime aspects, envied by terrestrials. Space will inspire a
stronger and finer man.
