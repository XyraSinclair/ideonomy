Current advocates of the colonization of Space have in Part intended such
colonies to represent replies or solutions to the limits to growth people.
The colonies are meant to illustrate that totally self-sufficient, nonpolluting,
closed-cycle, low-technological, &c colonies for large numbers of people can
quickly and easily be built in Space, and with exponential growth in number
and magnitude by self-reproduction. The colonization of Space would therefore
seem to enable the safe continuation of exponential population growth. The
population of Space is an excellent illustration of Ppronatalist reasons since
the increasing numbers of people would be desirable and critical in order to
raise the standard of living and cultural experience one would enjoy in Space:
the more people the better. People explore, build things, run things, maintain
things, create things, attend to one another, generate culture, complement
one another, &c. Furthermore, when one adventures into a frontier charged
with potentialities but vacant of men, and undertakes its development, the most
natural thing in the world is that population increase to Fill the new lebensraum.
