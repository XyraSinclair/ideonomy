and asteroids generally are mineralogically and otherwise interchangeable,
which would depress some or most of the immediate interest the jovian moons
might otherwise have. Mining of in situ materials for bases, colonies, and
other purposes on these moons will occur regardless simply as a matter of
convenience; in this case a certain question arises over the completeness

and complementarity of their spectra of elemental, chemical, &r mineral
Prevalence. The Solian plot of mass against density suggests that satellites
and asteroids diverge from one another as classes in terms of structure 6&r
composition, although the reason and significance are not understood. The
asymmetry of the Moon's subgeal and antigeal hemispheres--in terms of mass
concentration, figure, feature, &c--and suggestions of the same hemispheric
asymmetry for other planetaries, imply that planetaries--perhaps regardless

of their rotational-revolutional indices, at least presential--may not only
differ in these ways interhemispherically, but also in a much subtler and
antipodeally stratified way heliocentrically or planetocentrically, and be
valuable for mining in these dichotomic or bipolar terms; although the
question of qualitative and quantitative degree will be decisive. It deserves
reemphasis that the variety and idiosyncrasy of jovian planetosatellitic
systems, both within and between themselves and the Solar System as a whole,
may well turn out to be so great and important in terms of composition,
morphology, &r conditions that the Solar System will for all purposes deserve
to be treated as a medley of several solar systems as if the solar systems

of several surrounding stars were to be torn from the vault of the heavens

and juxtaposed within ours, perhaps even because aspects of variety between
satellites are as great or greater than those existing between the various
planets or per heliocentric distance, perhaps thereby--upon its discovery--
multiplying the difficulties and opportunities alike for any future Spacial
Program prosecuted by Earth, or favoring at least temporary concentration

upon the revelation and exploitation of an intersatellitic palette and realm.
