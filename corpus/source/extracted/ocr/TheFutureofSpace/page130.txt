"Artificial supermeteors'' are great objects or masses dropped purposely to
earth from Space by man in the future as a transportational short-cut or method
of engineering (as above). Theoretically they could range in size from those
pebbles of such density-with-mass as would slow their descent to a point that
undesirable decomposition or evaporation would not occur, or impactive
disintegration, or from 100-13 tons. In the latter case there might be warrant
for reducing their mass to 1-1,000 tons; above a 1,000 tons the atmosphere
would not slow them, and below a ton some floor exists to further atmospheric
deceleration, but over the specified range 200-fold deceleration of meteorites can
operate and maximize velocity at as little as 400 mph (these being suggestive
estimates) at impact. Excessively large (by which is meant enormous and
millennially rare) meteorites have never been recovered, their fate being unknown.
This commentary has concerned unshaped and unguided meteors, however. Oblique
trajectories, pelagic reception-points, and aerodynamic design for friction
or fluidity, bluntness, lift, &rc are also possible for perfecting delivery;
asteroids or fragments might even be selected for better properties! It is
unclear with what preciseness the path of bodies or showers could be predicted,

future. Asteroids existing naturally as, or made into, light foams could
