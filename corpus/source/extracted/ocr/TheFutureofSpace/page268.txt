REASONS WHY LIFE IN SPACE MIGHT ACTUALLY BE BETTER THAN ON EARTH

Foreword. There is a possibility, a probability, an argument, a position, a
belief, an idea, &r an opportunity (for making it so) that life in Space--in
colonies, bases, spaceships, &r homes in vacua or on planetaries--will sooner

or later, or particularly tater or Particularly at first, actually be better

than life on Earth as a whole or for some, as a whole or in particular respects
Or aspects. Such a claim is shocking to many persons who look at Space and

see nothing but hardship, difficulty, sacrifice, danger, risk, uncertainty,
discomfort, Spartan life, isolation, and cheap imitations of Terran existence,
who in fact see Nothing itself. It is even more Surprising and disputable

than the assertion that Spacial existence might equal or simply approximate

the excellence of life on Earth. Yet there have been those who have suggested
that one of the very reasons for colonizing Space is that life there

will be intrinsically superior to concurrent life on Earth, and perhaps inimitable.
If this were true it would have tremendous force in the argument for colonization
even in the near term. The following points are associable, dissociable,
amplifiable, extendible, mutually additive or multiplicative, variously weighted
and weightable (and in sundry respects), hypothetical, counterarguable, &r can

be seen in many different lights.

1. Income will be high, perhaps very high, in Space relative to Earth.

2. Innovation, invention, discovery, development, implementation, renovation,
&c will be higher.

3. Rate, degree, and scale of overall progress will be higher.

4, Excitement, drama, intensity, adventure, fascination, challenge, motivation,
justification for actions, &c¢ will be higher.

5. More interesting, intelligent, and better people will be attracted to and
concentrate in Space.

6. There will be greater freedom of enterprise, gain, government, taste, behavior,
Purpose, thought, values, ideals, creativity, initiative, belief, &c.

7. Space will be meritocratic and timocratic since mobility, power, profit,
recognition, prestige, help, opportunity, influence, & will be governed,
even very strictly, by skill, accomplishment, success, relevance,
intelligence, talent, utility, efficacy, experimentation, common evaluation,
the opinions of the best, individual Productivity, hard work, and other
virtuous things. Rationalism, Pragmatism, practicality, idealism,
personal integrity, science, technology, engineering, Progressivism,
experimentalism, imagination, perfectionism, communalism, rigor, natural
competition, evolutionism, intellectualism, tremendous incentives,
universalism, individual toughness, &c will dominate things, giving a
characteristic cast to Space.

8. A known renaissance will be taking place in Space, the birth of a new and
greater civilization, an opportunity for a transvalued and nobler man,
an ideal place for the peaceful and autonomous development of new and
higher societies (new and better governments, life-styles, Standards,
attitudes, interrelations, institutions, manners, goals, psychologies,
philosophies, economic systems, organizations, ethnic entities, religions,
jobs, life cycles, interests, friendships, cultures, atmospheres, &c),

a chance to leave the old and false behind, a chance for science and
technology to leap ahead and Prove and consummate themselves or acquire
their proper role in the scheme of things, an opportunity to equal

and surpass nature (its cycles, systems, resources, bases, faults,
limitations, dangers, overal] structure, evolution, complementarity to
man, &¢; its biology, geology, meteorology, &c; its sheer beauty), a new
and greater concern with posterity and ultimate things, an unprecedented
differentiation of humanity, &c. Never before will} any human renaissance
