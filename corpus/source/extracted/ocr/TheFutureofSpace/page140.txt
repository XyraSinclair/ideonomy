The relative &r absolute importance of primary, tertiary, and quaternary sectoral
activities may be the whole point or the winning argument for an economic
geosatellosphere; a manufactural sector might evolve derivatively from these

prior and basic activities, or play a very minor role over a longer term. It

is worth mention that the satellosphere could extend continuously and subtly into
Earth's atmosphere, even well below the traditional least orbit of about 100 miles,

esomotive or exomotive. Satelloids could actually operate at lower-atmospheric

rapid drop-off of density of the Earth's atmosphere (many orders of magnitude
greater than the additional atmospheric resistance caused by the difference in
flight velocity between an orbital object and usual jetcraft).
