Remarks.

The preceding distribution of men throughout the Solar System 75 years hence
in 2050 represents my intuitive estimation of what will actually be the case
at that time. Many considerations come to mind able to alter the relative and
absolute distribution in marginal and drastic ways. For example, populations
at other planets might be far more clustered and unequally distributed than |
have suggested above. Only token populations, representing little bases, might
exist at the outer planets. Lagrangian colonies might represent so little
advantage over epiplanetary and periplanetary loci that but 1% as many men
might be gathered at these loci as | explicitly and implicitly suggest. Epiplanetary
loci might so dominate colonization that satellospheres and Lagrangian points
will support but a tiny fraction of Spacial population. Countless possibilities
come to mind and defy perfect allocation. The actual distribution in 2025, 2075,
and so forth might differ radically from that fn 2050, perhaps as the result
of rapidly fluctuating and conflicting influences.

The distribution f have provided for 2050, in the face of these things,
simply represents a ''surprise-free projection'' in that its realization at that
time would not at all surprise me and would surprise me far less than many other
distributions, including those with which most people would probably be far
happier or which exist subconsciously in most people's minds.
