in intellectual power occurring with the development and application of humanly and

times by following, heliosatellitic, Mercurian-station, &rc relays. Why so probe
the Sun? There are recent signs we are fundamentally in error in our theory as

to the nature of its energies, structure, and processes, that the phenomena of

the Sun are far--perhaps arbitrarily--more detailed, complex, and exotic than we
naively assumed, and that the history and changes over time of the Sun are not

so simple, stable, and regular as we thought. It appears that Solar phenomena

have a highly critical, puzzling, and genuinely disturbing causal relationship

to meteorologic, climatic, and other patterns on Earth. Astonishingly, intelligence
should ultimately be able to alter, control, and transform these Solar effects and
causes, or the Sun itself, possibly at a surprisingly close date and very importantly.
