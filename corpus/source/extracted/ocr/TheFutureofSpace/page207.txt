PROBABLE SCENARIO. An attempt is made in this scenario to project the most
probable course of things, Say on the basis of a sophisticated outlook and
one whose basic assumptions have been defined.

OPTIMISTIC, VIGOROUS, AND YET NATURAL SCENARIO. Space is pursued at greater
than its current or moderate pace but on the basis of what is technically
and economically likely, perhaps in a way consistent with events and visions
of the future between 1957~1966/9.

OPTIMAL SCENARIO. The way Space ought develop. A judgmental scenario.

MAXIMALLY VIGOROUS AND YET NATURAL SCENARIO. Likely means, now and later,

are utilized to the full. Things are done as quickly and massively as possible,
especially by using means already available or creatable in 1975. A maximally
accelerated scenario.

FAR-OUT SCENARIOS. In these there enter critically important means, incentives,
and events which, however, Currently seem very unlikely, even by approximating
actual things. The critical entrants may be questionable in the sense that

very few people today would view them as very likely whereas some do, or because
really everyone agrees that they are unlikely. Discoveries, inventions,
unexplainable events, underived resources, spectacular human events, unexplained
velocities of progress, &rc might contribute to such scenarios. It should be
understood that, perhaps apart from specific or explained scenarios, there is

a very good chance that what will actually occur in the future of Space would

be placed by ourselves in the category of a far-out scenario.

PROGRAMMATIC SCENARIO. Here a Program is set forth as the bone &r the flesh
of what will occur in the future of Space as if the latter will conform to
a coordinated and logical plan.

PREPOSTEROUS SCENARIOS. Here the course of things is foreseen on fantastic,
ideal, fallacious, i}legitimately Supramaximal, &r just dumb terms, say like
a story told in a children's comic book. Many people in fact see the future
of Space in this way.

AUTOCATALYTIC SCENARIO. Here the stress of the portrayal is on showing how
developments will or might lead to developments so that the future of Space
will develop like the branching of a tree or a self-exciting chemical reaction.
Such autocatalysis might purposefully be overdone--by Overconnecting events,
making certain effects seem inevitable or overpowerful, limiting counteracting
events and consequences, smoothing the way for consequences, making branches
too numerous, abbreviating branches, anastomosing branches, compressing
branching, assuming perfect motivation, disregarding what is likely, &rc--for
such a reason as to impress the reader's mind with the fact that such
autocatalysis does occur, controls things, transforms things, &c.
