A LIST OF SOME FAR-OUT EVENTS WHICH MIGHT HAVE DRASTIC CONSEQUENCES

Foreword. The following events may be likely or very unlikely, or may be taken to
stand in varying degrees of analogy to everything else so far-out and critical
that is yet possible, but are all distinguished by having as their possible or
probable consequence the necessity of really radical &r drastic changes in our
picture of the future of Space. [n the author's opinion at least several such
events with miraculous consequences will in fact occur in the course of the

next 50-200 years and redirect the course of history; some of these can be
predicted as to their kind and effect, others really cannot be. The point is
that conventional and supposedly ''moderate'' views of the future of Space over

the long term are truly ''surprise-free'' in respect to their timing, scale,
selection of events, and other assumptions since the far-out events will probably
intercede and horribly degrade such simplistic "business as usual'' scenarios.

My list of these events is by no means exhaustive since it is the very nature

of the category that the events are less predictable and more surprising than
other events. In a certain sense the list which in effect exists in nature is
probably infinite, which my list by no means is.
