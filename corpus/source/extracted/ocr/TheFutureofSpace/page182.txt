almost entirely those of fuel, a predominance which might increase in the

future with riddance of wear, maintenance, and nonrecurrent costs) might

similarly be flattened, perhaps particularly for freight, in which case the

expenses involved in the shipment of biocultural products through Space might

be forgotten, leaving the other considerations reviewed, largely as if Space

were located on Earth, and vindicating the idea of Spacial production for

Terran needs and wants at points in the future.

curve in calculus: a conscious, unconscious, or virtual turning point in the

fate of the race may have transpired of indefinitely great importance in the

a critical realization or inflection. As an example, or by way of illustration,
