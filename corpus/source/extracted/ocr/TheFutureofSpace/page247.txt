THE VALUES OF SPACE

A justification for virtue and excellence,

Mystery.

Grandeur.

Excitement.

Adventure.

Challenge.

Beauty.

Scientific curiosity.

Profit.

Fun.

Daring.

Escape.

Imagination.

Inspiration.

Maintenance of frontier.

Human diversity.

Nobility of thought.

Unusual setting.

Human mixing.

Social experimentation.

Social segregation.

Political innovation.

Political independence.

Freedom.

Life purpose and direction.

A cause.

A sense of the miraculous.

A counterpole to life on Earth.

A clean and fresh existence; a new civilization.

An external and unifying challenge.

An alternative to Earth.

A protection against disaster on Earth.

A fabric of human purpose and a program.

Privacy.

Novel community and cooperation.

Pride of human accomplishment and capability, personal role, transcendence
of nature, complete artificiality of environment and support, ethnic
distinction, meaningful life, use of intellect and ability, independence,
enlarging goals, contribution to posterities, proven faith and determination,
pioneering spirit, architectic role, satisfied life, concertion, &c.

Zenithal ambition.

Familial cohesion.

Distraction from neurotic concerns, trivia, self, others, a static future,
Smug and closed life, a dead present, a nihilistic and effete zeitgeist,
an unchallenging life, a rotting or seemingly rotting world, and other
human discontent and unhappiness.

Peaceful, creative, and constructive existence,

Enterprise.

Distance from things.

Quiet, loneliness, thought, dream, wonder.

Distinction and identity.

A quest.

Meaning.

Discovery.

Innovation.

Self-sufficiency.

Expanded being.
