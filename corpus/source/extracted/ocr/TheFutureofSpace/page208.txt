WHY ENERGY HAS NO ABSOLUTE COST

It is ordinarily assumed that energy has some sort of absolute cost, an attained,
imminent, or eventual floor that wil] limit anything man can do, now or later, and
that confers absolute cost minima upon his actual and possible undertakings and
technology. This is not so. That it is not so is critically important since present
future possibilities in Space are basically limited or shaped by actual and
possible costs, amounts, kinds, and other dimensions of energy and power.

Government could take over the production of energy or fuel in order to eliminate
inessential profits and facilitate specific purposes. There is a temptation to
limit production, limit development and innovation, hold prices high, conserve
present means and ways, &¢ that pervades current industry. Direct costs of
producing energy are tied elaborately to the whole economy so that the role of
government could also be to minimize all related peripheral costs: the deflation
here could be enormous for these superimposed prices are themselves artificial and
inelegant. Energy produced can be reallocated to such a purpose as developing Space.
Government can subsidize fuel purchase. Moreover, extraordinary natural wealth and
opportunity is available for a development not currently pursued by industries
indifferent to any oceanic or cheap needs, and ‘certainly disinterested in diminishing
their returns per,peusd sale. Gross national Product, annual growth therein, &c
are totally misleading since the cost of energy is dependent on historical events,
arbitrary economic distributions and values, and variable exponential rates of
growth and rates extraordinarily dependent upon prior rates of investment and
overall economic emphasis. If man now explicitly undertakes to maximize energy and
growth of the relevant industry, science, and technology, transcendental exponential]
advance is possible in terms of deflated costs and prices, excellence of the form
of energy, marginal redistribution, momentarily usable energy, available stockpile,
&c. {tf 50% of working men worked on producing energy and developing energy resources,
disproportionately great deflation of future energy cost would result (availed
energy might even raise general social income despite the modification and
specialization of the workforce so that the situation is inherently surprising).

No supposed or actual limitations upon the rate of extraction and fixed supply

of natural energies really have any bearing upon the diminishability of energy

costs in connexion with facilitating Space. Assuming Space has returns, economic

&r energic, increased and cheapened energy for, Spacial enterprise would probably
encounter phenomena equivalent to phase transitions, and catalyses with future or
direct returns overwhelmingly enlarging returns, repaying investments, and operating
in terms of great and explosive positive feedback.

There are no absolute limits to human income, its rate of increase, or the warrant
for its increase. Certain scientific developments are imminent &r possible--
solar energy, fission, fusion, transnuclear processes, exomotive drives, more
efficient conversion, conduction, storage, &¢, &c--able to lower the cost of energy,
and increase the power of certain systems, by very many orders of magnitude. Any
such change would have nonlinear benefits of radical kind. Possibilities exist for
more isentropic systems that could recover energy and raise the efficiency of its
use, perhaps arbitrarily in terms of the endless development of scientific
discovery, and these would clearly diminish the energy and dollar cost greatly.

The value that man places upon goals and purposes in Space will be decisive in
determining the costs of what he does there and thus in varying optimal prices,

even apart from the diminishability of these costs as a result of industrial

and research investment or effort at redesigning the economic system. Certain
economies of scale inevitably do and would Operate because of which larger operation
of energy-producing processes would arbitrarily diminish energy costs over time.

energy benefits from means already provided. Men could labor to produce a focal
