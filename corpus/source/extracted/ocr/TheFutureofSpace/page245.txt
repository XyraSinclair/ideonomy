PURPOSES OF THIS BOOK

constitute the best, and an exhaustive, book on the future of Space.
make the perfect case for and against space.
be eminently fair.
list all possible events in the future of space.
supply these with some sort of ordering and thereby create something of a
calendar for what will happen in space by 2176 and beyond.
list all sorts of events which may be critical for what man does in space
by functioning as positive and negative catalysts, &c.
treat distant and fantastic possibilities.
present many convincing pictures for how space may develop.
show that life in space may be as meritorious as life on Earth.
show the impact on man of going into space.
attempt to foresee what will be found on other planets and what will develop,
generally, in space.
define the economics of space.
justify short-term accomplishments in space by relating them to greater things
that will eventually result and to a big picture of what space will finally
become.
furnish future possibilities in space with a useful vocabulary.
suggest new engineering possibilities.
provide a plan for how space should be investigated and develop.
allow better selection of alternative possibilities.
in effect, supply future spacial civilization with a voice in the self-centered
present.
show how science and technology will go on developing in a drastic way.
make the case for and against spacial industries.
depict the possibilities for man's continued exponential development.
provide an outline and handbook for further discussion, research, analysis,
synthesis, and projection, and a guide for simple action.
crystallize everyone's vague thinking about the future of space.
ignite a new interest in the development of space, to restore its support,
and to enlarge the undertaking.
