Appropriately enough, a proper ''second earth'' will exist in 2001. The X
term refers to an actually or facultatively self-subsistent colony branched

reproduced itself in the colony and be able to reproduce again in the future
through the same hereditary agent. The integrity of Terran culture, per se,

culture as an irreducible gestalt, monad, or hologram and so--assuming

event of foreseeable Terran catastrophe or self-annihilation. But one might
concern oneself further with creating such a colony as soon as possible,
designing it so as to minimize disruptive effects from Earth or to maximize
the rate at which the colony could recover following imaginable disruption,
