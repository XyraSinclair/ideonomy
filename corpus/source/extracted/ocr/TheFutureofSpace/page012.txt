TELEPORTATION Any technological analogue to the parapsychological concept
of material things being moved through space immaterially. Specifically,
telefacsimilization of objects reproducing to some degree their appearance,
constitution, or even chemistry--eg via 10°72 bits/gram--and invading
thereby the realm of so-called communication. The velocity of transmission
might range from greater than, at, or less than the speed of light. The
rapidity of terminal conversion might range from less than a second to
months. The presumable idea is that, with or without simplification,
the character of the object is transformed into, or transmitted as, pure
information &r energy, and is thus received and rematerialized. But
there exists the possibility that something like ''superspace'’ might enable
an object qua object to be shifted discontinuously and instantaneously
through space-time, sans strict conversion or any simplification.

SUPERSPACE A foundational manifold of our normal, and infinitely many other,
universes which the renowned physicist John A. Wheeler says must be taken
for granted. The concept breeds various and sundry remarkable, and indeed
bizarre, possibilities, whose nature and extent are highly imaginative
and but negligibly understood by modern physics and mathematics. For
example, there may exist some or infinitely many other universes (possessed
of arbitrarily different laws, energies, matters, dimensions, phenomena,
times, spaces, &c), these might be voyaged to (eg via black and white
holes), time itself may be travelable in either direction, and’so on
ad infinitum.

SUPERGRAVITY The hypothetical possibility that gravitation can be artificially
intensified, and thus applied.
