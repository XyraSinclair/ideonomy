WHICH WOULD ULTIMATELY BE A BETTER PLACE TO LIVE: ON EARTH OR IN THE SUN?
(A SERIOCOMIC JEU D'ESPRIT)

A fair idea might be had by tossing a coin into the air: heads for the center
of the Sun and tails for the surface of the Earth. The point is that we really
cannot know on the basis of current knowledge and preferences. By the time it
becomes possible for us to inhabit the Sun--and the time will come--it may be
that we or our distant offspring will be indifferent to the choice or greatly
prefer a habitat which presently seems the most intolerant of life, an "antihabitat".

Why the Sun might be Ideal for Life

There is plenty going on.
The interior of the Sun is more interesting than the surface of the Earth.
More energy is available.
Life there will be closer to the source of energy.
There is more room in the Sun than on Earth.
Natural phenomena may be more complex than on Earth.
There is more matter (eg higher density) in the Sun than on Earth.
Certain living processes may only be possible at the high temperatures found
within the Sun: the Earth may be far too cold to support ''life'',
Certain living, chemical, physical, and other Processes may only be possible
on the basis of the extreme Pressures found at the center of the Sun or
other stars; nuclear or seminuclear processes possible or natural there
may be far more intricate than any possible with matter limited to weak, vague,
and sluggish electron shells, for example.

Why the Earth might be Ideal for Life

Right chemicals for any life are confined to it.

Neither too hot nor too cold.

Not too radioactive, magnetic, violent, electrical, dense, gravitational,
changing, complex, uniform, &c.

Allows many states of matter (solids, liquids, gases, and plasmas) to coexist
and interact.

Permits the slow reorganization and evolution of matter.

Suitably bidimensional and environmental ly sharp-edged.

Chance precedents.

May have changed, and change, more drastically with time than the Sun.

It might be that it only seems so to human beings so far blind to utterly
unfamiliar chemistries, biologies, percepts, concepts, mathematics, &rc
such as may characterize what exists on and within the Sun.
