MICROATMOSPHERIC Having considerably less atmosphere, and atmospheric pressure
at ground level, than Earth.

PICTOMAP A photographic montage of the surface of a planetary substituting for
a customary map and for direct exploration, given its resolution and peculiar
character. Such a pictomap may be the combined product of innumerably many
and diverse instruments, surveys, computer analyses, human designs, &c. It
may, in fact, be an atlas and comprise many alternative versions or renderings
of the landscape. It may depict both morphology and chemistry of the surface,
and either stereoscopically or holographically. Its resolution may be
absolutely incredible, the kind of thing that can be restudied under a
microscope and imaginatively walked over and reconstructed. Such pictomaps
may be creatable for all planetaries in a short time, economically, and
with almost available technology. They may use passive &r active sensors
in orbit.

PERPETUAL MOTION DEVICES or INTERPLANETARY ORBITERS Devices repeatedly circling
between planetaries, and also Sun, by relying on their gravitational fields
as their motive power and on the frictionless vacuum of space.
