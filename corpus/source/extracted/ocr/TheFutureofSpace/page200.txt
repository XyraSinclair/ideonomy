EARTH

The good planet Earth, sun-
blessed sanctuary of life. travels
in tandem with its sterile moon.
Artist. Pesek imaginatively por-
trays them from the vantage point
of the asteroid Hermes, as it swept
to within a mere 500,000 miles of
Earth in 1937. So large is the moon
——a fourth Earth's diameter—that
many astronomers regard the pair
as almost a double planet. Here
the moon, closer to the asteroid,
appears abnormally large.
