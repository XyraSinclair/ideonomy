O'NEILL COLONY, TERRAN L-5, &C At two points 60° ahead of and 60° behind Moon
in its orbit about Earth dynamical stability exists with respect to the gravity
of these two bodies. These two Lagrangian points, L-4 and L-5, invite
colonization by man, as has been Proposed and developed by physicist Gerard
K. O'Neill. O'Neill visualizes them as populable by millions of men, as
sites for large-scale industries, and as way stations for other points in
Solar System. He specifically visualizes their emergence between 1982 and
2020. It is not inconceivable that considerable matter exists naturally at
these loci. This pair of points also exists for the Sun-Earth system, for
the Sun-Jupiter system (the Trojan Planets), &c.

ARTIFICIAL MOON A huge reflector distributed or erected in space in order to
redirect sunlight to Earth, eg to diminish nocturnal darkness, raise
temperatures at high latitudes, &c.

RERELUNAR Located on the backside of Moon.

SUPER ATMOSPHERES Atmospheres with supernorma] oxygen, pressure, or the like.
Hypothetically, these might improve agriculture when this is done off Earth

EXOBIOLOGICAL ACCIDENT Some disaster resulting from or involving extraterrestrial
organisms. An example would be a Terran pandemic produced by a pathogen
returned from a planet visited by man, cf The Andromeda Strain, a novel by
Michael Crichton. Conversely, a Terran Organism might ravage what biology
was previously found on the other planet.

HOLLOWED ASTEROID Dandridge M. Cole and Donald W. Cox, in their book Islands
In Space, The Challenge of the Planetoids, suggested that elliptical asteroids of
moderate size might be hollowed out, Spun, and inhabited. The spin would
give them artificial gravity, the mural landscape could be planted, farmed,
and built upon, an artificial atmosphere could be created, along with an
artificial sun down -the major axis; Such a nice intraspacial microcosm is
vividly depicted in Rendezvous With Rama, a novel by Arthur C. Clarke.

ARTIFICIAL PLANETS Buildings of planetary size (eg 1076-192 ) built at once
or incrementally in space out of available materials. Natural planets are
too dense and hot inside to be developed at great depth. So the Proper

way the inhabitable surface can be multiplied tremendously, eg 100,000 x.
