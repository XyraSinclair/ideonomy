The asteroids may be a source of carbon absent on the Moon, and other minerals
likewise. Recent evidence suggests that asteroids of the Belt are indeed highly
differentiated elementally, chemically, and mineralogically, even in terms of
Solar distance and to some extent in terms of size. Some three dozen different
types of spectra have been found, for example, and densities appear to vary
considerably. Recently the asteroids have been viewed in terms of past
condensation, agglomeration, accretion, heating, metamorphism, and differentiation
Processes in an effort to explain the accumulating facts. Their materials may
well include varying gaseous content. Asteroidal types may vary with the shape
and inclination of their orbit. Classification systems used for meteorites on
the Earth are considered to be suggestive of the nature of asteroids as well.
