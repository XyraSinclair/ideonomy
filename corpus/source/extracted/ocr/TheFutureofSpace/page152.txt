The first real hostilities in Space will occur before 2015. Let us face it, xX
this is not a surprise. Hostility is probably inherent in human nature. Over
time, the spectra of size, number, and kind of hostilities will inevitably
grow. They will likewise grow as human numbers, wealth, purposes, &c in Space
inevitably increase by many orders of magnitude. The very isolation, distance, cost,
size, strangeness, danger, &c of Space can and will serve as an inducement to
crime, hostilities, misbehavior, anarchy, emotional behavior, &c. There wil]
certainly be all kinds of discrete causes for the inception, growth, vigor,
continuation, and extension of hostility &c: vast but finite and competitive
wealth, opportunities for individual fortune and graft, patriotism, critical
essentials, &c. Nations, companies, and individuals will compete strenuously
and consciously for prior, best, and any minerals, energies, and loci. Everyone's
launches, stations, ships, bases, colonies, ideas, needs, purposes, &c will
compete. All sorts of administrations will be scattered over Space and inevitably
give rise to covert and overt hostility and competition, good and evil, jealousies,
strains, misunderstandings, misconceptions, worries, prejudices, escalable games,
conflicting ownership, other legal conflicts, traditions, prides, conflicting
jurisdictions, conflicting purposes, controversial extensions of jurisdiction
and power, critical habits, disagreeable procedures, disagreeable controls,
resented authority, conflicting perceptions of truth, good, and beauty; &c.

Initial and elementary differences will enlarge, transform, ossify, intensify,

and become independent forces of turmoil, hatred, bitterness, subversion, neurosis,
myth, and religion. The spectrum of human types will gradually slide into Space
and set up shop. Supplies can be misused and impropriated. Funds can be
embezzled, frauds can be perpetrated, lies can be told, favors can be paid for,
mistakes and consequences can be masked, conspiracies can arise, propaganda

can develop, deceits can be implemented, &c. Greed, jealousy, xenophobia,
antisociality, hunger for power, simple human fallibility, carelessness,
professional differences, psychological differences between men, &c will all

play their role in the creation and maintenance of hostility. The development

of government, and the changing aspect of Space overall, will unavoidably

give rise to hostility. Responsibilities in Space, and concomitant potentials

for guilt and resentment, will be enormous: human error may result in the collapse
of a colonial dome, collision of ships, pollution of the environment, squandering
or destruction of resources, exobiological havoc, death of dependents, awful
discomfort (eg offensive or unhealthy air), redistributions of wealth, major

costs or other burdens, &c. The countless unknowns, incessant development, scale,
fast development, and very intricacy of Space as an enterprise will often

lead to radical, important, and strategic disagreement among men, and scathing
tests. The fewness of men, meritocracy, turnover, load, enthusiasm, variety, 6c
of men in Space will result in severe tests in human relations and permanent
hostilities. Space may be invaded by Terran military purposes, by weaponry and
forces serving offenses and defenses on Earth, or conflicts limited to, or even
centered on, Space. The diversity and disconnexion of parties and authorites

in Space will make for at least initial anarchy and perhaps uncontrolled conflict.

it might, for example, initiate a century of conflict and barbarism. Even if

war and militaries are kept from Space, or deep Space, there wil} be crimes and
