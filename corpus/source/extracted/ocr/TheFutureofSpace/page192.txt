(4) Any perceived limitations of resources critical to the survival, happiness,

(5) It may at last dawn on mankind that, apart from theological supposition and
ignorant belief, the quantity of human beings is an absolute good, with the
profoundly important corollary that potential people have a right to exist, and

we, the preexistent and determinative, have an obligation to accomodate what

wealth of human beings are compatible with a relative excellence of contemporary
existence. At minimum this means profound analysis and profound courage must
precede acceptance of any status quo, particularly the level of humanity existing

on and off the Earth would seem today, and a fortiori tomorrow, able to accomodate

(6) Any current perceived or real limitations to the growth, size, and quality

This list could be extended and yet it seems to me that the point has been made

that victorious reasons exist for the circumstances imagined earlier in this

article on highly optimistic assumptions for the future growth of Spacial population
on spectacular scales and rates. It may well be that mankind is on the verge

of a downward inflection of the curve of growth of world population, a secondary
exponential process checking explosive procreation and stabilizing the world by
completing a rough logistic curve in the 21st century. Yet | have indicated above
that the history of such curves is endless reinflection and undulating graduation,
suggesting the manyness of horizons, the mountainous pleating, of Chinese paintings.
The most realistic expectation is, therefore, that the future of human population

in the years immediately before us will be such that it will lag and surge, lag

and surge, according to the challenges and opportunities of nature and the protean
metamorphoses of the human intellect. Any other expectation would be parochial.
