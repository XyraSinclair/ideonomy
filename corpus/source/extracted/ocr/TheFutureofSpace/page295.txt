PRONATALISM

Pronatalism is a favoring of human pullulation and a larger population.
It is obviously somewhat in conflict with the current antinatal philosophy.
Indeed, many people view anyone striking a pronatal stance as either
afflicted with a tragic religious bias or daffy. After all, the
assumption is that the Earth is drastically and increasingly overcrowded,
dangerous, polluted, underfed, and underresourced, and that mankind, stil}
expanding, is Proceeding toward some ghastly planetary crisis when the
limits to growth will be touched and the race decimated.

The reader of this book will be conscious that the writer is directed
by a different and far more optimistic view of things. He recognizes that
the world is Presently full of crises and undergoing a relatively
uncomfortable transition, but he sees these things as quite transitory and
the consequence of the world emerging from virtually medieval conditions
and advancing toward a higher and better civilization. He also regards
on a larger and more knowledgeable Prospect of the critical transition
being undergone by civilization. Failure to recognize this more basic
Prospect may complicate the metamorphosis and could well be disastrous.
Faith, confidence, and affirmation are necessary ingredients of a world
making the present transition. Pessimism and reaction could be deadly.

There are both trivial and profound reasons for enlarging population
now and in the future. These reasons have gane unconsidered by the current
one-sided mentality. The consequence of this may have been, and may continue
to be, untold moral harm without the slightest consciousness of the fact
on the part of ourselves. Al] the more reason, therefore, for Presenting
the pronatalist argument.

Not many years ago Europeans were gripped by a strange fear that falling
population would eradicate the race. Conditions in the world change.

Human attitudes even more so. The next age may reexperience the previous
fears of Europeans, especially since the tide of population may be ebbing

as a result of perfect, universal, and ''abused!! contraceptive technology

and as an overreaction against the present swelling of global population.

In addition, the trouble with current population size and growth may be
local, extrinsic, and fully solved in 30-50 years. tn 30-50 years the
technical and economic Progress of the world may provide the basis for

such wealth and control that the Earth may be in a position to accomodate
populations much vaster than any obtaining or foreseen now. The stupendous
human population existing in the world today would have been impossible
without the wealth created by past science and technology. There is every
reason to believe that scientific, technical, and economic progress wil]

in the future maintain Past exponential rates, and yet global population
growth is now about to decelerate of its own. As a result, relative progress
of science, technology, and the economy should assume a further, discontinuous
acceleration, and it is sensible to expect that many current problems wil]
rapidly diminish, especially since many of these problems are marginal and
temporary in nature anyway.
