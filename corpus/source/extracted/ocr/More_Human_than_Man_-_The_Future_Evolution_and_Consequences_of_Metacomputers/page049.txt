MILITARY USES AND EFFECTS OF A.1.

Uses

- Robot sentry.

Detection, deactivation, and removal of mines; intelligent mines;
shock-resistant vehicles.

Superhumanly fast, efficient, complex and adaptive fire control.

Intelligence collection, analysis and synthesis.

- Cryptographic-code generation, breaking and use.

Target recognition, discovery and tracking.

. Manless, wholly automatic tanks, planes, missiles, torpedoes, boats,

helicopters, artillery shells, trucks, ambulances, decoys, etc.
Planning, executing, integrating, criticizing, interpreting and
reacting to tactics and strategy; command and control.
Simulation and visualization of engagements, missions, battles,
theaters and wars.

Massive surveillance and reconnaissance by exotic sensors (passive,
active and mobile).

Multiform robot soldiers, teams and armies.

Smart handweapons, bullets and personal sensors.

"Expert systems'' as doctors, cartographers, weathermen, repairmen,
engineers, construction workers, linguists, accountants, etc.

Weapons invention, design, simulation and testing.

Training, perfecting and maintaining skills at every rank,

Ultra-mobile, ever-ready forces (day/night, all-weather, etc).

Contingency preparation and management.

Command and control of strategic nuclear forces (superior to man in
judgment, alertness, reliability, coordination, coolness,
indefatigability, invulnerability, total knowledge and expertise).

Automatic gunner and gun emplacements.

Active defense and instantaneous counter-fire.

Search, rescue and removal.

Counter-A,. 1.

Early-warning and pursuit systems.

‘Intelligent fences'.

‘Smart! communication networks,

Miscellaneous uses: automatic loading, co-pilots, semi-automatic
teleoperators, robot scout, fire fighting, friend/foe identification,
robot refuelers, terrain analysis, 'reactive jamming’, emergency
human backups, minimization of human risk-taking, ‘intelligent
and self-correcting’ maps, experience analysis, automatic briefing
and debriefing, readiness and performance evaluation, robotic
'field industries', etc.

Effects

- Greater or decisive power to the 'good guys' (Free World); relative

regression of the military power of the Communist World.

. Safer wars (fewer military widows)--and hence perhaps a greater

willingness of democratic nations to fight wars or use military
force; less vulnerable forces.

. Dangerous depersonalization of war, or even a loss of human control.
. Reduction of the value, point or winnability of wars--and hence

ultimately a more peaceful, less aggressive world; war made "absurd!',

- More intelligent, efficient, complete and flexible military control;

better strategy and tactics; better trained and educated forces.
