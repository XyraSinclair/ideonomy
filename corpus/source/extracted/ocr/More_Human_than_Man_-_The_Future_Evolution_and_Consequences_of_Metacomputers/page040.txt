POTENTIAL HUMANIST APPEAL OF A.J.

- Ability to enhance man's appreciation of nature.
- Robots liberating human workers from unpleasant, degrading, repetitive,

uncreative and dehumanizing jobs.

. Will free everyone of the necessity to work and enable a new and

permanent epoch of universal and absolute leisure.

- Will end man's exploitation of man.
. Will enable every Tom, Dick and Harry to be his own boss and sovereign.

Will put an end to the overregulation of modern life.
Will create infinite art and beautify the world.

- Will mean ‘technology with a human face' and rehumanize industry and

society.

- Will lead to 'kaleidoscopic' and 'bespeak' industry, and hence to an

infinite variety of personalized goods and services and a maximally
dehomogenized, variegated and pluralistic world.

Will give every man on earth the chance to live like a king, and the
wealth to do virtually anything.

Will catalyze a universal, perpetual and infinite efflorescence of
civilization.

Will rescue the world's poor from poverty, stagnation, ignorance and
misery.

. Will enable the life of the mind to be inconceivably exalted.
. Promises what is 'more human than man', or something equivalent to

an apotheosis of human nature.

- Promises the ultimate liberation of the human spirit.
