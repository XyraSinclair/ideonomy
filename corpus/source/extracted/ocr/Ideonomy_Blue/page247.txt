The interest and utility of semigeneric analogical traits is more various

'The predictive powers of semigeneric traits are diverse: they can predict

something hitherto undiscovered or even unconsidered about the central

analog to which they refer, or they can predict the behavior, form, qualities,

causes, effects, variations, relationships, elements, etc of their proximal
analogs. They can also predict things about whatever bears an arbitrary

relationship to the central analog, which is anything whatever.
