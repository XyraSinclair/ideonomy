Any Systematic Treatment of Ideas.

It is rare for ideas, or anything whatever, to be treated at all systematically—or as
systematically as typifies ideonomy’s approach to things. In fact, wherever ideas are dealt
with in a systematic fashion, this could be described as being ideonomic; and any book,
paper, definition, inquiry, or the like which thus systematizes concepts is, in effect, a bit of
ideonomy.
