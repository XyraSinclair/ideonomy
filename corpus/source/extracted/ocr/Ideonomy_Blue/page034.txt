{ "43 QUESTIONS TO ASK WHEN TREATING A PATH".§

Where does the path START?

Where does the path END?

What is ALL of the path; [where, what, or how long <absolutely and
relatively>] is it?

Is the path FINISHED or is it still [growing, changing, evolving,
or otherwise active]?

Did the path have to develop as it did, or COULD IT instead [HAVE
DEVELOPED in any OTHER WAY or have taken any other form] ?

[To what extent, in what way, and for what reason] does the path
VARY IN SPACE in [<external or internal> <structure or
appearance>, composition, location, environment, function,
e/vc]; and how uniform is it?

[To what extent, in what way, and for what reason] does the path
VARY IN TIME in [<external or internal> <structure or
appearance>, composition, location, environment, function,
behavior, requirements, e/vc]; and how uniform is it?

Is the path ALONE or are there any other paths?

Does the path BRANCH—or its branches branch?

Does the path [INTERSECT or join] any OTHER PATHS?

Does the path [intersect or ANASTOMOSE] ITSELF?

Does the path have a GOAL or does it seem goalless?

How does the path behave with respect to itself; does it INFLUENCE
ITSELF? .

Is the pathor ‘path traveling'~[AUTONOMOUS, heteronomous, or
both] ?

What are all ABSOLUTE ways of QUANTIFYING the path?

What are all RELATIVE ways of QUANTIFYING the path?

Does the path have a [sharp or definite] BOUNDARY-~or merely a
[diffuse, ragged, approximate, or indefinite] one?

What CAUSES the path; what [INFLUENCES or could influence] it; why
did it ORIGINATE; what [purposes, roles, functions, values,
uses, Or importance] does it have?

Is the path [CONTINUOUS or interrupted] ?

Is the path [VIRTUAL or substantial <a locus, thing, operation, or
function>] ?

Is the path [UNIDIRECTIONAL or bidirectional] ?

Is the path [PERSISTENT or transient]?

Is the path ITSELF [INTERESTING OR TRIVIAL] ?

How does the path RESEMBLE OTHER PATHS; what paths is it analogous
to?

How does the path DIFFER FROM OTHER PATHS, what paths does it
differ from, and how [ordinary and special] is it?

What [SPECIES and GENERA] of paths does the path [belong to or
resemble] ?

What are [all other PERSPECTIVES from which the path might be seen
and all the ways in which it might seem to vary]?

Does the path contain INTERNAL PATHS or is it itself [contained or
branched) from [LARGER OR HIGHER] PATHS?

What other (co-real) paths may the path have CAUSAL relationships
to?
