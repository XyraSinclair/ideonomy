INTRODUCTION

Illusions pervade the world and, in order for the revelation of truth
to continue, it is essential that they be discovered, investigated,
removed, or transcended wheresoever they lurk in nature, the human mind,
society, or the machinery of civilization.

Apatology is the term given in ideonomy to the systematic study and
treatment of illusions in general or of any illusions in particular. It
is coined from the Ancient Greek words apate (deceit or fraud) and
apatelos (deceitful, deceptive, illusory, or wily). Those who study or
apply the ideonomic division ILLUSIONS AND APATOLOGY in either a casual
or full-time way would be apatologists.
