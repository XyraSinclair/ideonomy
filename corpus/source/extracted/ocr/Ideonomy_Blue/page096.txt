One of the key things that makes ideonomy worthy of being considered a whole new
discipline is its altogether unique power to generate—en masse and in a seemingly
exhaustive and instantaneous way—ideas about anything whatsoever.

The production of ideas, or ideopoesis, can be augmented in this extreme way in man
alone, in man-and-machine interactions, and in machine alone. The last of these three
things—the automation of ideation—may be a form of artificial intelligence.
