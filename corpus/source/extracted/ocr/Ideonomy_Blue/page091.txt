“Mathesis Universalis”,
Or Leibniz’s More General Program

The German philosopher Gottfried Von Leibniz both invented and simply imagined
a number of systems for directly advancing thought. Collectively these are surprisingly
reminiscent of what ideonomy is supposed to be.
