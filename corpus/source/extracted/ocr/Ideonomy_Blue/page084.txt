Classification of Ideas (Ideotaxy)

One of the first things a nascent science does is to classify the various kinds of basic
entities it concerns (which for ideonomy would be ideas). This first ordering of Nature—
and self-ordering—effected by a new science can come as such a surprise (by
demonstrating the possibility of order where previously none had been contemplated), and
be so important, that it creates the misimpression that classification is [all or mainly] what
the new discipline is about. But the classification of ideas is for ideonomy just a necessary
preliminary that will enable it to do a hundred other things besides.
