There are also various things and concepts that can serve as brief definitions or
metaphors for what ideonomy is or can do. A list of such is given in Fig. 32,355. Some of the
listed things are meant to refer to the science as a whole, but others are certainly more
limited in their capacity or reference.

Although these words and phrases have an ability to give insight into or appreciation
of ideonomy in a quick, direct, effortless, memorable, and reassuring way, they are often
misleading, they are all imperfect, and none should be taken too seriously. Ideonomy is
ultimately its own best definition and metaphor.
