§ DISCUSSION, “The List-Organon” §

Anyone who encounters this book, or almost any bit of ideonomy, will
almost at once be struck by the heavy use of lists and of a list-like style. It
is a situation that demands an immediate explanation, if only to prevent
a grave and persistent misunderstanding.
