The innocently used expressions similarity and dissimilarity are
actually holophrases that [consciously and unconsciously] stand for a
welter—or what are probably an infinity—of different-but-related
generic concepts.

To date few of these have been officially [mamed, defined, or
differentiated], and in fact almost none of the schemata have been
developed that are virtually necessary to permit these canonical concepts
to be [discovered, conceptualized, named, defined, precised, classified,
coordinated, and systematically exploited for ideonomic purposes], in a
[satisfactory or even minimal way].
