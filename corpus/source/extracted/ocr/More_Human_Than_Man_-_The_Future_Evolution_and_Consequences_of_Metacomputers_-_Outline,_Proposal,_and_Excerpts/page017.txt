The subject is so unusual and complex, and we have so many ideas about
it, that we feel 415 pages would be a good size for a semipopular book,
or even for a popular best seller. Certainly the theme is sensational
and adequate to sustain reader interest.

We would like to have a year to write our book. However, were it the
wish of a publisher to compress this time, in order to beat some competitor,

we could probably halve it without doing an injustice to the subject or

grave damage to the literary style.
