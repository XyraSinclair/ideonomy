NOTE: Each time metamachines achieve one of the following things for the
first time it will be an impressive, important milestone. Those who
develop metamachines might deliberately seek any or all of these milestones
and use their successes, progress, and failures to analyze, measure, and
plan progress in the field. The rate of achievement of milestones wil]
increase both gradually and suddenly with the years; the time of accelera-
tion will probably forewarn that true A.I. is just around the corner.
