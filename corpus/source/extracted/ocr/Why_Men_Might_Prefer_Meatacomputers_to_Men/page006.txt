‘Dimensions of A Perfect Pet!!

(2)

Maximal enjoyment of being around humans and of being given attention
by them.

Not being such things as: noisy, a nuisance, dumb, neurotic, tense,

"spoiled, hostile, dull, inactive, ineducable, violent, wild or
uncontrollable, senescent, complaintive, distractive, repetitive,
predictable, buggy, ululative, furfuraceous, habitually shedding,
dirt-transporting, given to fighting other animals, clumsy, or
unsanitary.

Graceful, complex, diverse, and purposeful movement.

Tendency to resemble, complement, or imitate its owner(s).

Total uniqueness (for everyone on earth, or every pet).
