When a collineation of Generic Forms is found in MacSping, each
graph point corresponding to each Form can be assigned a different color to
distinguish that Form from its collinear fellows and from the sub-set of
Forms lying outside the collineation or comprising the background sea.

One can use the "Nearest Neighbor" function in MacSping to skeletally
interconnect the members of the collineation with prominent white lines
revelatory of its axial identity and sequential (transformational) nature.
The same function can then be used to reveal, but with contrasting dimmed
or colored lines, the connections of the highlighted collineation (monaxial
constellation) with the other Generic Forms.
