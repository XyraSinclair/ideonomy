POSSIBLE TRANSHUMAN DIMENSIONS

NOTE: Listed here are simply some of the dimensions that are more or less
basic and obvious. The ideal would be to list the purely orthogonal, or
maximally compact and exhaustive dimensions; but the ideal is not practical.
So what is listed here has varying degrees of overlap and analogy, and is
mainly suggestive in nature. In examining the list, each item should be
imagined as though the adjective ''transhuman'' preceded and modified it.
