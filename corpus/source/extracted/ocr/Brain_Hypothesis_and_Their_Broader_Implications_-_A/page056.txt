RAISG@S D' ETRE FOR NEUROSCIENCE INCLUDE:

To understand the basis of our knowledge and to precise and extend that
knowledge directly = epistemology.

To understand ourselves, ie. our motives, needs, complexions, behavior in
history, essences, behaviors, ideas, perceptions, limitations,
liabilities, interactions, mndane ideals, potentialities, aspirations,
variety, capacity, scope, and form ~ psychology, aesthetics, ethics,
Sociology, neurology, &c.

Curiosity about a superb mystery.

Human replacement, transcendence, and service to the infinite good.

To understand the relationship between man's and all other nervous systems;
to grasp man's nervous system.

To describe and understand animals’ nervous systems and behavior.

To control man, eg. for human happiness and excellence.

To cure, better, and prevent - medicine,

New means,

Better wedding of industry and the soul = redirected ethos,

Time’s tremulously compounding problems.

Effect of the recentering and extension of science and technology.
Human disagreement.

Man's desire to dispense with unleisurely work = servant intelligence.
Anticipatory prevention of abuse,

A new interest in the ratio between our ignorance and knowledge; a velleity
for wisdan.

Fear for the hell we are building about ourselves and the nursery thing
that confronts us in our mirror.

A labile desire to unite the beauty of nature with the means of technology;
far more than this, a sense of the possible perfection of the future,
and of the contimity of all being.
