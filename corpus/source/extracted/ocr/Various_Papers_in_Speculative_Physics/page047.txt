is meant to be used in the subsequent writing of a rigorous orismological

and arcological treatment of the conceptual, or the "'categoreal'', foundations

and directions of physics. Historically, and presumably eternally, physics

and metaphysics are circular phases of one another: the imaginative generalization,
Or categorization, can properly proceed only so far, whereupon it must return

utilitarian relevance (which selects things which might otherwise seem or be
arbitrary, yet which are fundamental to science, which is a servant of life)
and likewise this latter phase or enterprise (which can be simultaneous) can
and should proceed only so far, whereupon the data, axioms, postulates,
purposes, methods, conventions, etc. must be reviewed, new and greater concepts

must be created, and a new phase of metaphysical generalization or categorization
must ensue. Metaphysics is merely the territory of highest conceptual formulation,
activity, relevance, or invention, the Empyrean of categories, or that soaring
towards the gonotelic point at infinity whereat all concepts, categories, and details
