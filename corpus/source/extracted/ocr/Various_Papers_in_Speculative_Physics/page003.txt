| will develop in this paper a thesis which represents a deep, but very

Such a belief, if valid, would require that the pursuit of any ultimate
physical, mathematical, philosophical, or other truth--as something by nature
finite, absolute, eternal, universal, essential, perfect, supreme, complete,
etc. in unlimited degree--is, has always been and will always be, fallacious.

| believe that the prevalence of the doctrine which is the opposite and

| believe that the general adoption of my contrary doctrine will, to a cor-
